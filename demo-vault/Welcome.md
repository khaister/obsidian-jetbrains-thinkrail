# JetBrains ThinkRail

An Obsidian theme modeled on [ThinkRail](https://github.com/JetBrains/thinkrail). See also [[Showcase]].

## Text

Regular text is Geist at 14px. **Bold** is medium weight, *italic* uses the real italic face, and `inline code` uses JetBrains Mono at 13px.

> A blockquote keeps the body text size and gets a thin strong-border rule on the left.
